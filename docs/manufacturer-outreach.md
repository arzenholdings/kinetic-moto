# Manufacturer And Dealer Account Outreach

Goal: open legitimate dealer/reseller relationships for Kinetic Moto before listing real brand-name bikes as available inventory.

Use this as the operating tracker for outreach. Do not list a product as available until the brand or distributor confirms source, media permission, pricing, warranty path, and fulfillment terms.

The protected execution view is available at `/admin/outreach`.

## Outreach Positioning

Kinetic Moto is launching as an ecommerce-first electric motorcycle reseller with a local showroom/service-support path. The pitch is not "dropshipper." The pitch is:

- clean ecommerce storefront
- local rider consultation
- setup, assembly, and handoff support
- lead capture and follow-up workflow
- future brick-and-mortar/service support
- brand-safe product presentation

## Brand-Specific Notes

### E Ride Pro

Ask for:

- authorized dealer application
- Pro SR, Pro SS 3.0, Pro Mini availability
- dealer pricing and MAP
- media kit/product image permission
- warranty routing and dealer support process

Known public paths:

- `https://www.eridepro.com/contact-page`
- `https://www.eridepro.com/dealer-signup`
- `https://dealer.eridepro.com/pages/dealer-account-activation-guide`

### Rawrr

Ask for:

- Rawrr dealer account
- Mantis X Pro and Mantis Mini availability
- dealer margin/MAP
- dealer locator inclusion
- media kit/product image permission
- warranty and support path

Known public paths:

- `https://www.riderawrr.com/`
- `https://www.riderawrr.com/become-a-dealer`
- `https://erp.rawrrdealer.com/contactus`
- Email shown publicly: `sales@riderawrr.com`

Watchout:

- Rawrr's dealer page says applications need a physical commercial space with showroom capacity. Use a pre-application note first unless Ben wants to apply with the current Bellevue office and showroom-in-development plan.

### Surron

Surron has a messy public web footprint and many lookalike/scam domains. Do not list Surron products until an official distributor or authorized channel is verified.

Ask:

- official US distributor confirmation
- dealer onboarding path
- allowed product list
- warranty and parts process
- approved media use

Candidate public paths to verify:

- `https://surronmotors.com/become-a-dealer/`
- `https://sur-ron.us.com/contact/`

### Talaria
